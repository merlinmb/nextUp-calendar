mcmdhome Calendar

A personal, self-hosted calendar dashboard.

mcmdhome Calendar (also known as nextUp Calendar) is a self-hosted dashboard, built and operated by Mark Beets for his own private use, that combines Google Calendar and Microsoft 365 calendars into a single, lightweight web view.

What it does

  • Reads events from connected Google Calendar and Microsoft 365 accounts
  • Displays them together in one combined dashboard view
  • Exposes a token-protected JSON feed for the operator's own companion clients (such as dashboard displays)
  • Accepts external events pushed in through a simple HTTP API, shown alongside connected calendar data

Who it's for

This is a personal project, not a public or commercial service. It is used only by its operator and is not intended for sign-ups from the general public.

Sign-in is required to view or manage calendar data. Sign in to your dashboard.
